Jiaxin Yan received the B.S. and M.S. degrees from Shandong University of Technology in 2019 and 2023, respectively. He completed his M.S. degree under the supervision of [Prof. Cong Liu](https://novaresearch.unl.pt/en/persons/cong-liu/). He is currently pursuing the Ph.D. degree with the School of Control and Computer Engineering, North China Electric Power University, Beijing, China, and is a member of the [EPI Lab](https://energy-pi.com/en) (Van der Aalst Energy Process Intelligence Laboratory) under the supervision of [Prof. Long Cheng](https://longcheng.eu/). His current research focuses on process mining and smart energy systems.


Research Interests
======
- Process Mining
- Smart Energy Systems
- Deep Reinforcement Learning
